Tirunelveli Halwa

What is Tirunelveli halwa?

Our traditional sweets are slow cooked in small batches using recipes passed down through three generations of family cooks in Tirunelveli. We source wheat, ghee and jaggery from trusted local farmers and avoid preservatives so every box tastes the way it did at home.
